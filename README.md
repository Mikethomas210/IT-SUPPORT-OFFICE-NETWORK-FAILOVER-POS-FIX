# IT-SUPPORT-OFFICE-NETWORK-FAILOVER-POS-FIX
Real-World IT Support Fieldwork At Prestige Superstore: Restored office internet by failing over from MTN FibreX to VDT , and fixed a POS receipt printer at the cash point to keep sales runing


## Overview
In a live retail environment, I handled two critical IT support issues that directly affected business operations:
1. Office internet outage - switched ISP connection to restore connectivity
2. POS cash point failure - receipt printer not printing at the checkout

Both issues, if not fixed quickly, would have stopped sales and office work.

---

## 1. Internet Failover: MTN FibreX to VDT

### Problem
The office lost internet access. Staff could not work, and connected systems were down.

### What I Did
- Diagnosed the internet outage from the network rack.
- Identified the two available ISP connections: **MTN FibreX** and **VDT**.
- Removed and re-patched the cables from the MTN FibreX router to the VDT router.
- Re-connected the active ISP to the office switch to restore internet for all users.
- Verified connectivity across the office.

### Result
Internet was restored. Office operations resumed without prolonged downtime.

### Skills Used
- ISP Failover / Internet Troubleshooting
- LAN Cabling & Patching
- Router & Switch Connectivity
- Network Diagnosis

## 2. POS Cash Point: Receipt Printer Not Printing

### Problem
At a cash point, the receipt printer was not printing payment receipts. This caused delays at the checkout and risked customer dissatisfaction.

### What I Did
- Checked the POS terminal (FEC POS system) and the connected **Xprinter thermal receipt printer**.
- Checked power, USB / COM connection, and printer status.
- Diagnosed the fault and fixed the printer connection / settings.
- Tested by printing a test receipt to confirm sales could continue.

### Result
The cashier was able to print receipts again. Sales at that till continued without interruption.

### Skills Used
- POS Terminal Support
- Thermal Printer Troubleshooting (Xprinter)
- Hardware & Peripheral Support
- End-User Support
